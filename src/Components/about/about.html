Where Nour comes from

Nour means "light" in Arabic. We chose it because the way light moves across Alexandria's seafront is how we want our clothes to feel: easy, bright and made for daily life.

We are a small team based in Alexandria. We choose each item ourselves, check the fabric and fit, and keep prices fair so that looking good doesn't have to be a luxury.

Whether you're after a new pair of shoes, something to wear to work, or the one accessory that finishes an outfit, we want the whole experience to be simple from the first click to the knock at your door.
